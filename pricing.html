What affects the final price?

Scope

Number of pages, modules, user roles, workflows and administrative features.

Integrations

Payment providers, SMS, email, third-party APIs and existing systems.

Delivery requirements

Content preparation, data migration, hosting, training, security and support.

Domain registration, hosting, paid third-party services, SMS charges and payment-provider fees may be separate unless explicitly included in a quotation.
